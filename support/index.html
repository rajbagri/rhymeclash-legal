Common questions

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Settings in the app and tap Delete Account & Online Data. This permanently erases your online profile, rank backup and scores. You need an internet connection.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings, then Manage Subscription, or iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Ranked or friend battles won't connect

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center and online, and that both players run the latest version.
