We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, advertising ID or email address. There are no ads and no tracking. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing. Your Game Center name and scores appear on public leaderboards to other players.

Who processes your data

Supabase (database and servers, located in Tokyo, Japan) and Apple (Game Center and the App Store). Your data is therefore stored and processed outside India. We share it only with these processors, to run the game.

Your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

You can ask for a summary of your data, correct it, erase it, withdraw consent, nominate another person to exercise your rights, and complain to us first and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Online features (sign-in, leaderboards, ranked and Daily play) require you to be 18 or older, or to have a parent's or guardian's permission, which the app asks you to confirm. We do not track, profile or show advertising to anyone, including children. Practice mode works offline and sends no data. A parent or guardian can email us to have a child's data erased.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), stored in an access-controlled database, and scores are verified on the server. If a breach affects you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Changes

We will update this page and its date. Material changes will be shown in the app.
