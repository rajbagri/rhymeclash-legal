Who processes your data

Supabase (database and servers, located in Tokyo, Japan), Apple (Sign in with Apple, Game Center and the App Store) and Google (Sign in with Google). Your data is therefore stored and processed outside India. We share it only with these processors, to run the game.

Your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

You can ask for a summary of your data, correct it, erase it, withdraw consent, nominate another person to exercise your rights, and complain to us first and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

  • Delete everything: in the app, Settings, then Delete Account & Online Data. No email needed.
  • Other requests: rajbagri3214@gmail.com.

Children and age

India: Rhyme Clash is for players 18 and older. The app asks you to confirm your age before you sign in. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we do not process children's data without verifiable parental consent, so players under 18 in India should not use the app.

Other countries: we follow the rules that apply where you live, including Apple's age rating and its Game Center and account age requirements. We do not track, profile or show advertising to anyone, including children. A parent or guardian can email us to have a child's data erased.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), stored in an access-controlled database, and scores are verified on the server. If a breach affects you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.
