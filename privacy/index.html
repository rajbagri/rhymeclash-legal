Ad measurement (Meta)

We advertise Rhyme Clash on Instagram and Facebook. To learn whether those ads work, the app includes Meta's SDK (app events only, no Facebook Login). It tells Meta Platforms when the app is installed and opened and when an in-app purchase is made, along with basic device details (model, iOS version, language, time zone) and a random ID the SDK creates. It does not receive your name, email, account, display name or game data, and it does not receive your advertising ID because the app never asks for tracking permission. Meta reports ad results to us only in aggregate, through Apple's SKAdNetwork and Meta's Aggregated Event Measurement. Meta processes this data under its own Privacy Policy.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase: our database and servers, in Tokyo, Japan.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and the App Store (including payments).
  • Google: Sign in with Google.
  • Meta Platforms: ad measurement only, as described above.

Your data is therefore stored and processed outside India. We share it only with these processors, for the purposes above.

Your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

You can ask for a summary of your data, correct it, erase it, withdraw consent, nominate another person to exercise your rights, and complain to us first and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

  • Delete everything: in the app, Settings, then Delete Account & Online Data. No email needed. This erases your profile, scores, tournament entries and reports from our server.
  • Other requests: rajbagri3214@gmail.com.

Children and age

India: Rhyme Clash is for players 18 and older. The app asks you to confirm your age before you sign in. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we do not process children's data without verifiable parental consent, so players under 18 in India should not use the app.

Other countries: we follow the rules that apply where you live, including Apple's age rating and account age requirements. We do not track, profile or show advertising to anyone in the app, including children. A parent or guardian can email us to have a child's data erased.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), stored in an access-controlled database, and scores are verified on the server. If a breach affects you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.
